Fees

Every program, every dollar.

CNA Program

$2,200 tuition fee package
$575 initial payment on enrollment Balance over 3 installments
  • Registration fee non-refundable
  • Program fees
  • Live Scan fingerprint fee
  • BLS/CPR course by American Heart Association
  • Review course
  • Uniform scrubs
  • Loaner textbook, training equipment & handouts

To enroll: valid/current driver’s license or State ID, and Social Security card/number.

HHA Program

$600 total
$200 due to enroll $400 due end of program
  • 5 class days of theory and clinicals
  • Loaner textbook & program handouts
  • HHA certificate

To enroll: active/current CNA license, valid DL or State ID, and physical exam & TB test within the last 6 months.

CEU Classes

$40 / $80 per class
  • 4-hour class (8:00 am – 12:30 pm): $40
  • 8-hour class (8:00 am – 4:30 pm): $80
  • Two topics per 8-hour class
  • CEU certificate issued after each topic

Active/current CNA or HHA license required. Cash payments preferred for CEU classes.

RNA Program

$350 total
$100 due to enroll $250 due end of program
  • 2 class days (Sat & Sun)
  • Program handouts
  • RNA certificate issued by QCHF
  • CEU certificate for 8 credit hours toward CNA renewal

To enroll: active/current CNA license and CNA work experience in a SNF.
