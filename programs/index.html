Program Details

Nursing Assistant (CNA)

Our CNA curriculum is CA State approved and designed to equip students with essential nursing skills, principles of care, and the knowledge needed for effective resident and patient care in various healthcare settings.

  • Length 22 class days (8 theory + 14 clinical)
  • Leads to CA State Competency Exam → CNA license (CDPH)
  • Requirement Pass school finals, both Written and Skills
  • Tuition $2,200 package · $575 due on enrollment · 3 installments

Home Health Aide (HHA)

Designed specifically to equip CNAs with the knowledge and skills to provide personal assistance and basic nursing care in patients’ homes — working with a home health agency or in a hospice setting. Theory plus clinical training in long-term care. An active CNA license is required to enroll.

  • Length 5 class days (Sat–Sun)
  • Leads to CHHA license (CDPH) + Certificate of Completion
  • Bonus 24 CEU credit hours toward CNA renewal
  • Fees $200 to enroll · $400 at end of program

Continued Education Units (CEU)

Essential for CNAs and HHAs maintaining licenses: CNAs need 48 CEU credit hours (recommended 24/year, minimum 12 in the first year); HHAs need 24 (12/year required). Renewals are submitted via the CDPH online portal.

  • When In-person classes every month, Saturdays
  • Options 4-hr class $40 · 8-hr class $80 (two topics)
  • Requires Active/current CNA or HHA license
  • Payment Cash preferred for CEU classes

Restorative Nursing Program (RNP)

An advanced certification course for CNAs interested in Restorative Care: theory and practical applications essential for therapeutic rehabilitation and career growth in long-term care.

  • Length 2 class days (Sat & Sun)
  • Leads to RNA Certification issued by QCHF
  • Bonus 8 CEU credit hours toward CNA renewal
  • Requires Active CNA license + SNF work experience
