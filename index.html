About Us

Here to educate and challenge.

Founded in 2010, Pacific Health Educational Center (PHEC) is a State-approved Training Provider by the California Department of Public Health (CDPH). Our school is centrally located in San Diego, providing convenient access to both North and South counties.

Our team consists of highly qualified and experienced DSD Instructors (RNs, LVNs), who facilitate academic exploration and support individualized learning through quality education. As a State Authorized Test site for CNAs, PHEC has consistently maintained high passing rates, attributable to the expertise and dedication of our Instructors and Staff.

Do I need experience to enroll?

No. You need to be 16 or older, have a valid State ID or driver’s license, a Social Security card or number, a high school diploma or GED, English comprehension in reading and writing, and a physical exam with TB clearance.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. The CNA program is $575 due on enrollment, with the balance of the $2,200 package split across 3 further installments.

Where do I take the state exam?

Right here. PHEC is a State Authorized Test site for CNAs, so you can take your California State Competency Exam in the same rooms you trained in.

Weekday or weekend: which is right for me?

Both run the same 22 class days (8 theory, 14 clinical). Weekday sessions run Monday–Friday; weekend sessions run Saturday–Sunday. Check current start dates for both.

What if I want to become a nurse later?

CNA training is often the first step toward a career in Nursing, Healthcare, or other Medical occupations, with real clinical hours and hands-on patient care experience that carries forward into LVN or RN programs.
